Privacy Policy – Scan2Excel

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. About Scan2Excel

Scan2Excel helps users extract information from document images and organize the extracted information in Google Sheets.

2. Document Processing

Scan2Excel uses Tesseract.js to perform OCR (Optical Character Recognition) in the user's browser. Document images are processed locally by the browser-based OCR functionality. The app does not use an external paid OCR provider in its current version.

3. Google Account and Google Sheets

Users can connect their Google Account through Google OAuth. With the permissions granted by the user, Scan2Excel can access Google Sheets to save or retrieve the records requested by the user.

Google account access is subject to Google's privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage

Records saved through the Google Sheets integration are stored in the Google spreadsheet selected or configured by the user. Google account data is handled through Google's authorized APIs.

5. Data Sharing

Scan2Excel does not sell users' personal information. Data may be processed by Google services when users choose to use Google Sign-In and Google Sheets integration.

6. Security

Users should protect their Google Account credentials and review the permissions they grant to Scan2Excel.

7. Revoking Access

Users can revoke Scan2Excel's Google Account access through their Google Account security settings.
